# Privacy Policy

**Vedi — Vedic Astrology Tool**

_Last updated: 6 July 2026_

Your privacy matters. This policy explains what happens to the information you
enter into Vedi ("the Service"). The short version: **everything stays on
your device.**

---

## 1. The Core Principle: 100% Client-Side

Vedi is a fully client-side web application. All astrological and astronomical
calculations run **locally in your web browser** using WebAssembly. There is **no
backend server, no database, and no API** that receives your data.

## 2. What We Collect

**Birth details you enter** (date, time, place, latitude/longitude, timezone,
ayanamsa, and any partner details for compatibility):

- Are processed **entirely within your browser**;
- Are **never sent to us or any server**;
- Are **not stored** by us, and are discarded when you close or reload the page
  (unless your browser is set to retain form fields).

**We do not collect, store, log, sell, or share any personal information.**

**Anonymous usage statistics** (page views, approximate region, device/browser
type) may be collected via Google Analytics — but **only if you opt in** through
the cookie banner. See Section 4. These statistics are aggregate traffic
measurements and are never linked to the birth details you enter, which never
leave your browser.

## 3. Local Storage

The Service may use your browser's `localStorage` only for **non-personal
preferences**, such as:

- Your chosen theme (light/dark);
- Your chosen interface language;
- Your cookie-consent choice (whether you accepted or declined analytics).

This data stays on your device, is not personal information, and is never
transmitted.

## 4. Cookies & Analytics

The Service uses **Google Analytics 4** to measure site traffic — visit counts,
approximate region (derived from IP by Google), device and browser type, and
which pages are viewed. This helps us understand how the Service is used.

**Analytics is opt-in.** We use Google Consent Mode with all storage denied by
default:

- **No analytics cookies are set unless you click "Allow analytics"** in the
  cookie banner shown on your first visit.
- If you **decline**, no analytics cookies are ever set. The Service works
  identically either way.
- Your choice is remembered on your device. To change it later, clear the site's
  browser storage (or the `trytellme_cookie_consent` key in `localStorage`) and
  the banner will ask again on the next visit.

What analytics **never** sees: the birth details, names, locations, or any other
information you enter into the Service. Those are processed entirely in your
browser and are never transmitted (see Sections 1–2).

Data collected by Google Analytics is processed by Google under the
[Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). You can
additionally block Google Analytics in any browser with the
[GA opt-out add-on](https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout).

The Service does not use advertising cookies or any other third-party tracking.

## 5. Hosting & Server Logs

The Service is served as static files from a hosting provider (for example
Vercel). Like virtually all web hosts, the provider may automatically record
standard technical request logs (such as IP address, browser type, and timestamp)
for security and operational purposes. This is handled by the hosting provider
under its own privacy policy, not by us, and is **not linked to the birth details
you enter** (which never leave your browser).

## 6. Children's Privacy

The Service is not directed to children and does not knowingly collect any
information from anyone, including children.

## 7. Your Rights

Because we do not collect or store your personal data, there is no personal data
for us to access, correct, export, or delete. You remain in full control of any
information on your own device and can clear your browser's storage at any time.

For optional analytics (Section 4), you control collection entirely through the
consent banner: it is off unless you opt in, and you can withdraw consent at any
time by clearing the site's browser storage.

## 8. Data Security

Since your birth details never leave your browser, they are not exposed to
transmission or server-side breaches through the Service. As always, keep your own
device and browser secure.

## 9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by
the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of the Service after changes
constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

## 10. Contact

For privacy questions, open an issue at <https://github.com/tapiocaboy/vedi>.
