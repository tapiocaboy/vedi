## 1. The problem this chart actually has

The ascendant is at **29.06° of Simha**. The sign boundary is 0.94° away.

| Birth time offset | Resulting lagna |
|---|---|
| −8 min | Simha 27.42° |
| −4 min | Simha 28.24° |
| **0 (as recorded)** | **Simha 29.06°** |
| +4 min | Simha 29.87° |
| **+5 min** | **Kanya 0.0°** |
| +8 min | Kanya 0.69° |

A recorded time of 19:15 is a five-minute increment, which is how clock times get written down. The gap between the moment of birth and the moment someone reads a clock and writes a number is routinely a few minutes. The AA rating certifies that 19:15 is what the certificate says. It does not certify the certificate to the minute.

So this chart has two live readings, and nothing in the source data settles which. Everything below is presented against both.

**What does not change:** all planetary longitudes, all nakshatras, the entire Vimshottari dasha timeline, and both divisional lagnas. The dasha timing analysis in section 4 is unaffected by the ambiguity.

## 7. Caveat

Three analyses in, the pattern across Diana, Obama and Jobs is consistent. Dasha *timing* keeps landing on real inflection points, and it is the one layer that survives every data problem, because it depends only on the Moon's longitude. House-based interpretation is where the failures cluster, and it is also the layer most vulnerable to input error and to boundary cases like this one.

For this chart specifically: the mahadasha structure is striking. Ketu, a 7-year period, contains the founding of Apple, the Apple II and the IPO, and nothing else in his life is compressed like that. Venus, a 20-year period, contains the ejection, the wilderness, the marriage, Pixar and the return, which is exactly the shape of a long chapter. Sun, six years, holds the peak and the diagnosis. Moon opens in March 2008 and he was visibly ill by that June.

That is either a real signal or it is what happens when you divide any 56-year life into nine unequal segments and go looking. I cannot distinguish those two possibilities from a single chart, and neither can anyone else working retrospectively.
