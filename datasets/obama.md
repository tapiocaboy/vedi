| Ketu | (2nd) | 2nd, Dhanishta | Neutral | Detachment from family wealth and inherited standing |

**Three structural facts:**

1. **Shasha Yoga.** Saturn in own sign in a kendra is one of the five Pancha Mahapurusha yogas, classically associated with command over others and durable authority. This is a legitimate prospective marker of eminence, not a retrofit.
2. **Strength is concentrated, not distributed.** Saturn at 16.4 and Moon at 15.0 sit well clear of everything else. The remaining seven planets cluster between 8.9 and 13.3. This is not a broadly strong chart; it is two very strong placements and a lot of ordinary ones.
3. **The 10th house itself is empty and its lord is in a dusthana.** Tula is untenanted and Venus, the 10th lord, sits in the 6th. Eminence markers are present; conventional career-house strength is not.

**Rahu in the 8th in Magha, with the 8th lord Sun in the 7th.** The 8th governs records, origins and what is hidden. Magha governs lineage and ancestry. Rahu governs distortion and foreignness. This is the birther controversy in one line, and it is the most on-the-nose placement in the chart.

## 4. Period boundaries, isolated

Most retrospective astrology works by matching a planet's significations to an event, which is easy because every planet carries enough meanings to fit almost anything. The constraining evidence is different: when a period *boundary* lands on a dated event that was not chosen after the fact.

| Boundary | Date | Event | Gap |
|---|---|---|---|
| Jupiter mahadasha begins | 24 Jul 1996 | Elected to first public office | 3.5 months |
| Jupiter-Venus begins | 3 Jun 2004 | DNC keynote address | 7.5 weeks |
| **Jupiter-Sun begins** | **1 Feb 2007** | **Presidential candidacy announced** | **9 days** |
| Jupiter-Moon | 20 Nov 2007 to 20 Mar 2009 | Campaign start through inauguration | Brackets both ends |
| Saturn mahadasha begins | 24 Jul 2012 | Re-election campaign, won 6 Nov 2012 | 3.5 months |

**Base rate, so this is not oversold.** Vimshottari produces 44 antardasha boundaries between birth and age 65, one every 17.7 months on average. A ±3.5 month window around each boundary covers roughly 39% of the whole timeline, so a hit at that tolerance is close to a coin flip. A ±2 week window covers about 6%.

That reframes the table. Rows one, two and five are weak evidence individually. The Jupiter-Sun row at nine days and the Jupiter-Moon bracketing are the only entries that clear the base rate by a meaningful margin. Two genuinely striking coincidences is a fair summary, not five.

| Mars conjunct Rahu in the 8th is classically severe for longevity and accidents | No such event | Miss |
| The 10th house is empty and the 10th lord is weakened | Two-term head of state | Miss |
| Export prose: "Mercury is this chart's structural pillar ... 9.5/20" | Mercury is the second-lowest scorer; Saturn is 16.4 and vargottama | **Residual bug.** The selector appears to rank by count of divisional own-sign or exalted placements rather than by Vimsopaka score, and contradicts its own number in the same sentence |

**What the chart does get right prospectively.** Shasha Yoga and an exalted Moon in Rohini in the 5th are both genuine classical eminence markers, present from birth and not dependent on hindsight. A practitioner in 1990 could reasonably have called this a chart for public prominence. What no rule here supports is the specific claim of head of state, and the placements a practitioner would have flagged as limiting (debilitated Jupiter, yogakaraka in a dusthana, empty 10th) are precisely the ones the biography contradicts.

## 6. Caveat

This was fitted with the biography in hand. The classical rules failed on the chart's headline features roughly as often as they succeeded, and the boundary evidence in section 4 shrinks to two solid entries once the base rate is applied.

No forward-looking claims are made. Obama is living, the analysis stops at the documented public record, and dasha periods beyond 2022 are included as arithmetic rather than as prediction.
