## 0. Data correction

The first export set the timezone field to `UTC`, and a subsequent attempt used GMT+0. Britain observed British Summer Time from March to October 1961, so 19:45 on the record is UTC+1. Casting the chart for 19:45 UT rather than 18:45 UT advances the ascendant by roughly 43° at this latitude and date, which crosses a sign boundary.

| Export's D10 text: uniformly positive career reading | Never notices the 10th lord is in the 8th | Still wrong |
| Export's Wealth footing: Weak, −2.33 | Settlement reported around £17m | Miss on outcome, though it tracks her documented anxiety about money |
| Export's Health footing moved from −3 to −0.8 | Bulimia, self-harm, depression | Moved the wrong way |

Two structural observations about the tool:

1. The D9 and D10 narrative modules read planetary dignity by sign only. Sign positions never depended on the ascendant, so correcting the timezone did not touch them. They were wrong before and remain wrong, for a different reason. The Vimsopaka table is byte-identical across both exports for the same reason.

2. Every genuinely strong indication in sections 3 and 4 came from analysis the export does not perform: maraka lordship, Sade Sati, transits over natal points, eclipse contact, and pratyantardasha resolution. The export stops at mahadasha level and contains no ayurdaya section.

## 6. Caveat

This analysis was fitted with the biography already in hand. The house placements under Vrischika lagna are strikingly apt, and the Rahu–Mars marriage timing is a clean hit on a period boundary that was not chosen after the fact. That is a different claim from saying the chart would have called any of it in 1980.
